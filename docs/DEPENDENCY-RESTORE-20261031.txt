Local adaptations:
- MediaBitmapCapabilities.java requires explicit authenticated CAP,COVER,1 or
  CAP,CROSS,1 messages before optional image processing/transmission is enabled.
- The reviewed 1.75/1.85B receivers only implement Bulk types 1 through 4.
  Cover/type 6 and Cross/type 5 remain disabled on those receivers. This change
  restores complete source dependencies; it does not add firmware decoders or
  promise artwork/junction bitmap display on existing boards.
- Asynchronous encoders reject superseded work after a hide/clear/new image.
  Capability notifications are scoped to the current authenticated connection.
- The standard edition retains complete RLE maps. Its pre-existing missing
  onZmapCapability callback was removed, and its missing onNack callback now
  resends the current complete map when the packet type/id match.
